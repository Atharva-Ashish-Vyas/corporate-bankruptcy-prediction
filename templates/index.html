Questions worth asking

Why not just use accuracy?

Only about 4.7% of statements have a bankruptcy label. Always predicting non-bankruptcy looks accurate but misses every bankruptcy.

Is the score a probability?

No verified probability claim is made. The model scores are uncalibrated; use them to understand model ranking and the threshold decision.

What are the limitations?

Historical Polish data may not generalize to other countries or periods. Company identifiers are unavailable, so exact deduplication cannot ensure company-level separation. Missing values and class imbalance also affect results.
