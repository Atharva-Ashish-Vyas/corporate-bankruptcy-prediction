03 / REPRODUCIBLE METHOD

How the experiment works

  1. Prepare the dataset. Remove exact duplicate statements, preserve missing ratios, and reserve 20% as a stratified test set.
  2. Compare six classifiers. Fit preprocessing inside each development fold and select the highest mean average precision.
  3. Choose the threshold. Maximize F2 using development out-of-fold predictions, then freeze the model and threshold.
  4. Evaluate once. Report the test confusion matrix, precision, recall, average precision, and bootstrap intervals.

Historical data may not generalize to another period or country. Exact duplicate removal cannot verify company-level separation because company identifiers are unavailable.
